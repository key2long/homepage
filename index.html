I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). My research sits at the intersection of knowledge graphs and large language models: I build models that represent, retrieve, and reason over structured knowledge — from diffusion-based knowledge graph completion and complex logical query answering to evidence-path reasoning that grounds LLM answers in verifiable facts.

I am particularly interested in how models hold uncertainty about what they know, when they should retrieve rather than generate, and what makes a reasoning path trustworthy.

Research Interests

  • Knowledge graph embedding and completion
  • Knowledge graph question answering
  • Large language model reasoning
  • Diffusion models for structured data
