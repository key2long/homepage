About

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I work on knowledge graphs and language model reasoning.

My research sits at the intersection of knowledge graphs and large language models. I build models that treat knowledge as something to be generated, embedded, and reasoned over — from diffusion-based knowledge graph completion and complex logical query answering, to evidence-path reasoning that grounds LLM answers in verifiable facts.

I am especially drawn to questions that sit between representation and reasoning: How should a model hold uncertainty about what it knows? When should it retrieve, and when should it generate? What makes a reasoning path trustworthy?
